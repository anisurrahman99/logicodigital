How a project actually runs.

Five stages, each one ending in something you can see — not a status update.

01

Discover

We audit your current site, your competitors, and your goals, then write a short brief we both sign off on before any design work starts.

02

Design

Wireframes first, then high-fidelity screens in Figma. You review and comment directly — nothing goes to development until you've signed off.

03

Build

One- to two-week sprints, with a staging link you can check at any time. No black-box month of silence.

04

Launch

Cross-browser and cross-device QA, analytics and tracking wired up, and a rollback plan in place before we flip the switch.

05

Grow

Monthly reporting and a prioritized backlog of improvements, based on how real visitors actually use the site.
